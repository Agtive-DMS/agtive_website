1. Introduction

Agtive ("Agtive", "Platform", "we", "us", "our") is an agri-commerce and agri-services platform operated by ____________________ accessible via ____________________ (website) and ____________________ (mobile application) (collectively, the "Platform"). Agtive connects Farmers, Dealers, Sub-Dealers, Retailers, Warehouse and Logistics Partners, Influencers/Agri-Advisors, and Partner and Non-Partner Sellers, to enable the discovery, listing, sale, purchase, storage, and delivery of crop-nutrition, fertilizer, crop-protection, and other agricultural and allied products — both from the Agtive (Agtive.in) and from third-party non-partner brands listed on the Platform.

We know how much trust you place in us when you share your personal data, and we take that responsibility seriously. This Privacy Policy describes, in detail, the personal data we collect from or about you, how and why we collect it, how we use and share it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights available to you. By accessing or using the Platform, or by providing your personal data to us, you agree to the collection, use, storage, disclosure and other processing of your information as described in this Policy.

This Privacy Policy applies to all categories of Platform users described in Section 2, unless the personal data relates to a current, former or prospective employee of the Agtive, which is governed by a separate internal HR privacy notice. This Policy should be read together with our Terms of Use and any category-specific onboarding agreement (e.g., Dealer Agreement, Seller Agreement, Warehouse Partner Agreement, Influencer Agreement).

3. Our Commitment to Compliance

Agtive endeavours to comply with applicable Indian data protection and privacy laws, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made thereunder, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), as and when it comes into force, together with any sector-specific regulations applicable to the sale, storage, and distribution of agricultural inputs, fertilizers and crop-protection products. This Policy has a defined scope and application, and the specific rights and obligations described herein may vary depending on your user category, the products involved, and your jurisdiction.

4. What Personal Data Do We Collect?

"Personal data" means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data. Once you provide us your personal data, you are not anonymous to us. Where possible, we indicate which fields are mandatory and which are optional — you may always choose not to provide optional information, though this may limit your ability to use certain features. We collect the following broad categories of personal data (see Appendix A for detailed, category-wise examples):

4.1 Information You Give Us

  • Identity and contact information — name, mobile number, e-mail address, residential/business address, village/taluk/district/state, date of birth, photograph.
  • Onboarding, KYC and verification information — Aadhaar (where legally permitted and with consent), PAN, GSTIN, trade/storage licence numbers, bank account/UPI details, cancelled cheque, business registration and authorised-signatory documents.
  • Farm and agronomic information — landholding size and location, crop(s) grown, soil and irrigation details, purchase and product-usage history, advisory queries raised with us or with agri-advisors.
  • Trade and commercial information — outlet details, product catalogue, pricing, stock/inventory data, sales and order history, credit and payment terms, invoices.
  • Logistics information — facility address and capacity, vehicle and driver details, consignment and dispatch data, insurance details.
  • Promotional information — social-media handles, follower/engagement statistics, content submitted, referral codes, and payout details.
  • Communications — content of messages, calls, chats, reviews, ratings, survey responses, complaints, and feedback you share with us or through community/discussion features on the Platform.

4.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device and technical information — IP address, device identifiers, browser type and version, operating system, mobile network and carrier information.
  • Usage information — pages/screens viewed, search and browsing behaviour, clickstream, session duration, referring/exit URLs, app crash and performance logs.
  • Location information — approximate or precise geolocation (where permitted by your device settings), used for delivery routing, dealer/warehouse mapping, and localised recommendations.
  • Cookies, SDKs and similar tracking technologies — see Section 6.

4.3 Information from Other Sources

  • Our logistics, payment, and KYC-verification partners (e.g., updated delivery status, address corrections, identity/credit-verification results).
  • The Agtive and other group/associate companies with whom you may already have an existing relationship (e.g., an existing Agtive.in dealer profile).
  • Publicly available sources, government or regulatory databases, and business directories, used to support dealer, warehouse and seller verification.
  • Other users — for example, if a Dealer refers a Farmer, or a Warehouse Partner submits consignment details relating to a Retailer.

5. How Do We Collect Your Personal Data?

We collect personal data in the following ways:

  • Directly — when you register, complete onboarding/KYC forms, place or fulfil an order, contact customer support, respond to surveys, or otherwise provide information to us.
  • Automatically — through your use of the Platform's website, mobile application, and related services, using cookies, SDKs, and similar technologies.
  • Indirectly, from third parties — as described in Section 4.3, or where permitted or required by applicable law or regulatory requirements, we may collect information about you without directly seeking it from you.

6. Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies

We use "cookies" and similar technologies on certain pages and screens of the Platform to help us analyse web/app page flow, remember your sign-in and preferences, measure promotional effectiveness, personalise content, and promote trust and safety. "Cookies" are small files placed on your device that assist us in providing our services. Most cookies are "session cookies" and are automatically deleted at the end of a session; others persist to remember your preferences across visits.

You are always free to decline or delete cookies through your browser or device settings, although you may then be unable to use certain Platform features, such as adding items to cart, checking out, or staying signed in. Certain pages of the Platform may also contain cookies placed by third parties (for example, analytics providers such as Google Analytics), over whose use of cookies we have no control. You can learn more about, and opt out of, Google Analytics at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

7. Why Do We Use Your Personal Data?

We use personal data to operate, provide, and continually improve the Platform and the services offered through it, including to:

  • Create and manage user accounts, and onboard Farmers, Dealers, Sub-Dealers, Retailers, Warehouse/Logistics Partners, Influencers, and Sellers, including identity, KYC and eligibility verification.
  • Enable listing, discovery, ordering, invoicing, payment, fulfilment, and delivery of products (both Agtive / Agtive.in products and third-party partner and non-partner products).
  • Facilitate warehousing, inventory management, and logistics coordination between Sellers, Warehouse Partners, and delivery partners.
  • Provide personalised crop-nutrition and agronomic recommendations, offers, and content relevant to your farm, region, or trade.
  • Calculate and process referral, commission, and incentive payouts to Dealers, Sub-Dealers, Retailers and Influencers.
  • Communicate with you regarding orders, deliveries, account activity, promotional offers, newsletters, and Platform updates, via phone, SMS, e-mail, WhatsApp, or in-app notification.
  • Provide customer and partner support, troubleshoot issues, and resolve complaints and disputes.
  • Detect, prevent and investigate fraud, credit risk, counterfeit products, and misuse of the Platform, and assess creditworthiness where credit or deferred-payment terms are offered.
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulatory requirements (including fertilizer and agri-input trade regulations), and requests from government or law-enforcement authorities.
  • Conduct internal research and analytics — on an aggregated or anonymised basis wherever possible — to improve Platform features, user experience, product assortment, and business operations.

8. Your Consent

We collect, use and disclose your personal data only where we have a valid basis to do so — typically your consent, or as necessary to perform a contract with you (e.g., fulfilling your order), or to comply with a legal obligation. Depending on the sensitivity of the personal data, your consent may be express (e.g., a KYC declaration you sign or click-accept) or implied through your continued use of the Platform after being notified of this Policy.

Certain onboarding flows (Dealers, Sub-Dealers, Retailers, Warehouse Partners, Influencers, Sellers) may require additional, purpose-specific consents — for example, for credit checks, GST invoicing, or payout processing — as part of the relevant onboarding agreement. Where we intend to use your personal data for a materially new purpose not disclosed at the time of collection, we will seek your consent before doing so.

You may withdraw consent previously given by writing to us at the address in Section 21, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. Withdrawal of consent will not be retroactive, and may limit or prevent our ability to continue providing certain products or services to you.

9. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Data?

Information about our users is an important part of our business, and we are not in the business of selling personal data. We share personal data only as described below, and with recipients who are required to protect it consistently with this Policy:

  • Within the Agtive — with Agtive India Pvt. Ltd. and other group/associate/sister companies, for account management, cross-platform services, credit assessment, and coordinated agri-input distribution.
  • Transactions with counterparties — for example, a Farmer's delivery address shared with the fulfilling Dealer/Retailer/Warehouse Partner, or a Seller's dispatch details shared with the assigned logistics partner, so that your transaction can be completed.
  • Partner and Non-Partner Sellers — with the seller whose products you purchase, to the extent necessary to fulfil, invoice, support, or service your order. Products from non-partner (third-party, non-Agtive) brands are subject to that brand's own terms and privacy practices, and Agtive is not responsible for such independent practices — see Section 14.
  • Third-party service providers — payment gateways and processors, KYC/identity-verification agencies, logistics and courier partners, cloud hosting and IT service providers, SMS/e-mail/WhatsApp communication providers, analytics providers, and customer-support vendors — engaged strictly to perform functions on our behalf, under confidentiality and data-protection obligations, and prohibited from using your data for their own purposes.
  • Influencers / Agri-Advisors — limited to referral and attribution data necessary to calculate commissions or payouts.
  • Legal, regulatory and safety reasons — to comply with applicable law, respond to government, regulatory or law-enforcement requests (including subpoenas or court orders), enforce our Terms of Use, or protect the rights, property, or safety of Agtive, the Agtive, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganisation, restructuring, or sale of assets or business involving Agtive or the Agtive, in which case your personal data will typically be one of the transferred business assets, subject to the transferee's agreement to protect it consistently with this Policy.

Other than as set out above, you will receive notice when your personal data might be shared with third parties for a materially new purpose, and you will have an opportunity to choose not to share such information, where feasible.

11. How Secure Is Your Personal Data?

We design our systems and processes with your security and privacy in mind, and maintain reasonable physical, technical, procedural and organisational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the personal data we hold, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using industry-standard protocols.
  • Access controls that limit personal data access to personnel and service providers who need it to perform their functions.
  • Adherence to applicable payment-industry security standards for payment card and payment-instrument data.
  • Periodic review of our security practices as our Platform and services evolve.

Despite these safeguards, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We may occasionally request proof of identity before disclosing personal data to you, and we encourage you to protect your account credentials and sign out after using shared or public devices.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, to comply with our legal, accounting, tax, and regulatory obligations (including any record-keeping requirements applicable to fertilizer and agri-input trade), or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Where personal data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable law. We may retain data in anonymised or aggregated form for analytical and research purposes without further restriction.

You have the following choices and rights in relation to your personal data, subject to applicable law:

  • Access, correction and updates — you can access, correct, and update much of your personal data directly through your Account/Profile settings on the Platform.
  • Deletion — you may request deletion of personal data that is not otherwise required to be retained by us for legal, regulatory, or legitimate business purposes.
  • Withdrawal of consent — as described in Section 8.
  • Marketing communications — if you do not wish to receive promotional e-mail, SMS, WhatsApp, or push notifications, you may opt out through your Notification/Communication Preferences in the Platform, or by using the unsubscribe link in our communications.
  • Cookies — you may control cookies through your browser or device settings, as described in Section 6.
  • Advertising preferences — as described in Section 15.
  • Data portability — where required by applicable law, you may request a copy of certain personal data you have provided to us, in a structured, commonly-used format.

To exercise any of these choices or rights, please write to us at ____________________, clearly describing the personal data and the action you would like us to take. Please note that certain choices — for example, withdrawing KYC consent as a Dealer, Sub-Dealer, Retailer, Warehouse Partner, or Seller — may affect our ability to continue onboarding or transacting with you on the Platform.
